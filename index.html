About me

My engineering journey began in 2012, when my middle school robotics team won an award for the most innovative and creative design at the Tech Museum’s Tech Challenge. I returned to robotics in high school, where our team placed third out of 24 schools at UC Berkeley’s Pioneers in Engineering competition. I then attended Laney College, where I worked while studying and joined the MESA program for academic support, before transferring to San José State University and earning my Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering.

I'm the type of guy that wants to know things from the ground up, learning and building skills to become the best that I can be in order to build the best product possible!
